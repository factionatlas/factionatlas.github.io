Faction map

The descent tree of every faction in the atlas, drawn the same way as the layered faction chart. Each row is one generation below the deepest parent. A faction's wings sit inside its box; lines show descent, second parents and recorded ancestors. Recorded relations between factions can be drawn on the same tree. The text version is the faction tree.

    Legend

    • Faction, in its branch colour (grey: outside the left, center and right; light grey, italic: historical; dashed outline: provisional)
    • A faction drawn as a box holds its wings
    • + "+ 12": a closed branch with 12 factions beneath it
    • Columns: left, center, outside the left/center/right, historical camps, and the right. Rows: generations of descent.
    • Primary parent: the line of descent (solid, in the branch colour)
    • Wing of a parent drawn outside that parent's box (thin, dashed)
    • Second parent (magenta, dash-dot)
    • Co-equal parents (near-black, solid)
    • Recorded ancestor (descent) (dark, dotted)

    Relation lines (tick them under "Relation lines" above):

    • Alliance (solid, thick, green)
    • Friendly (solid, green)
    • Neutral (dotted, grey)
    • Feuding (short dashes, orange)
    • Hostile (long dashes, thick, red)
