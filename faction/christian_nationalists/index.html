Christian Nationalists

LAYER ALIGNMENT · FIRST SEEN 2026-10-15

Active

Mostly Reformed/Protestant pastors and writers who argue for a Christian nation with Christian magistrates. Stephen Wolfe's 'The Case for Christian Nationalism' (2022) is their key text. 11-02: the Sey deportation split widened. Supporters of CN in principle now say they oppose the 'NXR/Ogden variety' (ReformedCaio) or call NXR 'Nationalists without the Christian part' (Boots1689); TomBuck calls it 'rot' in the movement. 11-05 note: Wolfe (2022) and Isker's Boniface Option (2023) date from a more unified CN period; theonomy vs natural-law was then an argument among friends and has since escalated into a sharper present-day divide for placement.
