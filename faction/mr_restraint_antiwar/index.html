Ron Paul / restraint antiwar right

LAYER ALIGNMENT · FIRST SEEN 2026-10-20

Also listed as: MR: Ron Paul / restraint antiwar right

Active

The restraint antiwar right is the Ron Paul tradition of "a peaceful foreign policy and the protection of civil liberties at home", holding that "only through a peaceful foreign policy can we hope for a prosperous tomorrow."

Its main institution, the Ron Paul Institute for Peace and Prosperity, a project of Paul's Foundation for Rational Economics and Education, says it "continues and expands Dr. Paul's lifetime of public advocacy for a peaceful foreign policy and the protection of civil liberties at home" and that "A prosperous America is profoundly linked to a foreign policy rooted in peaceful relations and trade with all. With peace, comes real prosperity" (about[1]).

Its advisory board crosses party lines: Reps. Thomas Massie, John Duncan Jr. and Walter Jones Jr. (Republicans), Dennis Kucinich (a former Democratic presidential candidate), Judge Andrew Napolitano, Lew Rockwell and David Stockman. No membership test is given. Atlas notes: Older antiwar libertarian-conservatives who criticize the Iran war and US support for Israel without the DR's ethnic framing.

Examples: Ron Paul on Trump urging Netanyahu to 'finish the job' in Iran (post[26]); Scott Horton: 'Supporting Israel is against America's national interests' (post[27]); Ron Paul Institute on Vance's 'deceptive antiwar' speech (post[28]).
