Old Glory Club & heritage men's fraternities

LAYER ORGANIZATION · ERA 2022–present · FIRST SEEN 2026-10-21

Also listed as: AF: Old Glory Club & heritage men's fraternities

Active

The Old Glory Club (OGC) network of regional 'heritage American' Christian men's fraternities (chapters or 'societies').

On Oct 19-20 OGC disaffiliated at least two chapters: the Nathanael Greene Society of North Carolina (post) and the Lewis and Clark Society of the Rocky Mountains, which says it happened after it sent an open letter asking what OGC is, 'who is in it and who is out' and what chapters get from central (post). OGC gave no public reason in the saved files or today's reads.

Onlookers offer conflicting, unverified Israel-related explanations (post). William Wolfe publicly 'avows' the NGS (post). Follow-up (10-21/22): the Lewis and Clark Society's president says OGC 'kicked me and my entire chapter out with no warning just for asking questions' (post). Cody Libolt relays that 'the reason going around is that some members went to Israel' (hearsay, post).

OGC and the Indiana chapter still posted no statement (free read r02 finds only a meme reply from @OldGloryClub, post). The NGS carried on with its 'Greene Tavern' show, ep. 88 (post). Day 4 (10-22/23): the Nathanael Greene Society turned down the NNA's invitation: it 'has never held to civic nationalism or propositional nationhood… championing heritage Americans' (post).

A post-left account claims the club split along Catholic/Protestant/'Nazi'/secular/Jewish lines (unverified, post). OGC still posted no statement.
