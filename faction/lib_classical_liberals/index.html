Free-speech Enlightenment liberals

LAYER ALIGNMENT · ERA c. 2015–present · FIRST SEEN 2026-10-30

Also listed as: LIB: Free-speech Enlightenment liberals

Active

People and outlets that call themselves classical liberals or defenders of free speech and Enlightenment values, critical of illiberalism on both left and right; several do not call themselves right-wing.

The camp's best-known shared statement is "A Letter on Justice and Open Debate" (Harper's, 2020-07-07, letter[1]), signed by about 150 writers including Bari Weiss, Steven Pinker and Jonathan Haidt: "The free exchange of information and ideas, the lifeblood of a liberal society, is daily becoming more constricted".

It warns of "ideological conformity" and "an intolerance of opposing views, a vogue for public shaming and ostracism", names "the forces of illiberalism" on the right ("a powerful ally in Donald Trump") as well, and holds that "The way to defeat bad ideas is by exposure, argument, and persuasion, not by trying to silence or wish them away"; "We refuse any false choice between justice and freedom".

Quillette describes itself as "politically non-partisan" and relying on "reason, science, and humanism as our guiding values" (about[2]). Self-labels: Michael Shermer, "promoting free speech & classical liberal (I used to call myself a libertarian…)" (post[3]); Cato, "the classical liberal case for dispersing power" (post[4]), attacking NatCons for "abandoning free markets and limited government" (post[5]).

The letter is an open letter by individuals, not a document of this Atlas faction.
