Governing-wing moderate Republicans (Governance Group)

LAYER ORGANIZATION · ERA 1995–present · FIRST SEEN 2026-11-01

Also listed as: MR: Governing-wing moderate Republicans (Governance Group)

Active

House Republicans in the self-described moderate caucus, the Republican Governance Group, chaired by David Valadao (CA-22) (web). Membership comes from the Wikipedia roster (a self-chosen caucus affiliation, recorded in office.caucuses); it is not a judgment from posts. Most members are also in the Main Street Caucus, which Wikipedia calls a mix of 'moderates and party-line Republicans' (web), so Main Street membership alone is recorded but does not place anyone here. The allied Republican Main Street Partnership 'has moved to the right' since 2024, according to the NYT as cited on Wikipedia (web).
