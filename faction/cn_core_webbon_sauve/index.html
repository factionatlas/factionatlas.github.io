New Christian Right

LAYER ORGANIZATION · FIRST SEEN 2026-11-05

Active

Shared parent for two closely aligned networks that call themselves Christian nationalist and the "New Christian Right": Joel Webbon's NXR / Right Response Ministries (Georgetown, Texas) and Brian Sauvé's New Christendom Press circle (Ogden, Utah). They are separate organizations run by different people but extremely well aligned: wings of one core, not rival camps.

Webbon's conference opening speech "Defining the Movement" (November 2026, video[1]) describes a "nucleus" of four "pillars":

  1. "anti-Zionism" and "national sovereignty" ("We are America first"), including opposition to what he calls "Jewish supremacy, especially in non-Jewish places"; he is not anti-war in general ("We have our hemisphere")
  2. "masculine leadership" against "boss babe feminism"
  3. "race realism" and "pro-white" (he rejects "race essentialist" and "white supremacy" as descriptions), with an immigration moratorium
  4. "Christ is King" as a political claim, with "formal, official, unapologetic national deference" to Christianity.

He defines a nation as "more than blood and soil … but not less", rejects "propositional nationhood", and holds that God's covenant people are gathered "not by blood, but through faith". Allies "orbit the nucleus"; he allows for "those who are further to my right" but says the "actually hateful" are "not on this team". Outside critics often name the two wings together (e.g. "NXR and Ogden").

The Moscow/Wilson dispute (the "Ogden–Moscow controversy") is held at this level because it involves both wings.
