Libertarian Party strategy

LAYER ORGANIZATION · ERA 1971–present · FIRST SEEN 2026-11-01

Also listed as: LIB: Libertarian Party strategy

Active

Libertarians whose strategy is the Libertarian Party itself: LP national and state organizations, LP candidates and the people who promote them. The party says that, founded in 1971, it "provides a political home for all Americans who cherish freedom" and calls itself "the third-largest political party in the United States" (about[1]).

Its platform says: "we seek a world of liberty: a world in which all individuals are sovereign over their own lives"; its Statement of Principles says members "challenge the cult of the omnipotent state and defend the rights of the individual" and deny any government the right to violate the rights to life, "liberty of speech and action" and property; and its stated goal is "nothing more nor less than a world set free in our lifetime" (platform[2]).

Its About page adds positions such as "taxation is equivalent to forced labor", "We demand an end to the Federal Reserve", "We stand against the war machine", and that government's "only responsibility, if any, should be protecting people from force and fraud".

Membership turns on a certification: "I hereby certify that I do not believe in or advocate the initiation of force as a means of achieving political or social goals" (Libertarian Party of Ohio, membership[3]); LPedia says the statement is required by Article 4 of the national LP bylaws (LPedia[4]). Example of this faction's activity: LP National candidate interviews (post[5]).
