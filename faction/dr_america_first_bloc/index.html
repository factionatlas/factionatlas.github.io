America First

LAYER ALIGNMENT · ERA 2017–present · FIRST SEEN 2026-11-01

Also listed as: DR: America First

Active

In Nick Fuentes's own words, America First "means every other country is put last, every other country is not considered." Asked whether the current is "just" anti-Israel, he says that is "not enough" and states their own reason: "Why are we anti-Israel? We're anti-Israel because it's a foreign lobby.... So the center, the nucleus of it is nationalism and identity."

He also names trade (tariffs and reindustrialization) and immigration restriction as further "pillars of America First" (19:08–22:08[13]).

In his NXR conference speech he names the coalition itself: "The alternative to Peter Thiel and JD Vance and the Claremont Institute and Palantir and the Israel lobby is the Groypers, the Christian nationalists, the immigration restrictionists, the paleocons, the real America First from 2016." (30:04[1]). Name note: the same phrase is also used on the MAGA side.

The America First Policy Institute, a MAGA policy shop, says "America First is more than a policy agenda. It is a commitment to the conviction that the American people come first" [14]. A bare "America First" bio is not by itself grounds to place someone here rather than in MAGA.

'America First, not MAGA' self-labels are spreading among small accounts ahead of the midterm: 'I'm not MAGA, I'm America First. Trump is MAGA, and I'm not.' (post[15]); 'Maga is garbage... I'm America first... I will not be voting' (post[16]); 'I'm sitting it out because he betrayed us. I'm America First. Not Israel... First.' (post[17]).

Former Rep. Greene's bio reads 'AMERICA FIRST AMERICA ONLY' and she calls the midterm 'a referendum on Trump, Republican failures' (post[18]). The label is contested: Loomer also calls herself 'America First' while voting straight GOP.
