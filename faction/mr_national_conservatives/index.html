National conservatives (NatCon)

LAYER ALIGNMENT · FIRST SEEN 2026-11-01

Also listed as: MR: National conservatives (NatCon)

Active

Institutional nationalist conservatives built around the NatCon conferences (since 2019; London, Washington, Rome, Orlando, Brussels, Miami, Jerusalem) and Yoram Hazony's Edmund Burke Foundation (web). Hazony's bio: 'The Virtue of Nationalism... Host @NatConTalk | Trustee @Heritage'; he says 'Liberalism is perfectly capable of justifying a dictatorship of the "Enlightened."' (post) and promoted the NatCon Jerusalem talk 'Biblical Nationalism is Not for Israel Alone' (post). Heritage president Kevin Roberts: 'Globalism has failed. Markets don't magically transform people into Americans' and welcomes the VP 'speaking openly about restoring American nationalism' (post). Small accounts self-label too: 'a national conservative in the America First tradition' (post); a Hungarian analyst: 'I am a national conservative as far as domestic politics go and a hard-core realist' (post). Rivals and critics: Cato says 'NatCons... want to use government power to impose their nationalist agenda, abandoning free markets and limited government' (post); a founding-principles (Jaffa/Claremont) writer contrasts Jaffa with Deneen and worries about NatCon's view of the founding (post); Reference: a pro-Israel critic lumps 'The Groyper/NatCon movement' together (post), but NatCon's Jerusalem conference and Hazony's ties to Israel make it distinct from the groypers. Speaker lists (Hawley, Vance, Rubio, DeSantis, Thiel, Roberts, Tucker Carlson, Orban, Meloni) are co-appearance only, not membership.
