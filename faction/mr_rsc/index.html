Republican Study Committee (RSC)

LAYER ORGANIZATION · FIRST SEEN 2026-11-09

Also listed as: MR: Republican Study Committee (RSC)

Active

The Republican Study Committee describes itself as "the conservative caucus of House Republicans and a leading influencer on the Right since its original founding in 1973" (about[2]).

It says it "exists to bring like-minded House members together to promote a strong, principled legislative agenda that will limit government, strengthen our national defense, boost America's economy, preserve traditional values and balance our budget." It gives members "tools and research" and "a forum for like-minded members to join together to support common causes and challenge the status quo".

Its stated beliefs: "the appropriate role of a limited government is to protect liberty, opportunity, and security"; "more government is the problem, not the solution"; and "with conservative commitment today, America's brightest days lie ahead." Wikipedia calls it the largest ideological caucus in the House Republican conference; August Pfluger (TX-11) has chaired it since January 2025 (Wikipedia[1]).

Most also sit in other factions, and many Freedom Caucus members are RSC members too.
