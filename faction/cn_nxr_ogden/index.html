Joel Webbon's organizations in Georgetown, Texas. Right Response Ministries (RRM) is the ministry brand and NXR ('New Christian Right') the political brand.

'NXR' is also the name of Webbon's political YouTube channel, NXR Studios, whose hosts include Dale Partridge, Calvin Robinson and Harrison Smith. Hosting a show does not by itself place someone in this wing. Positions seen in posts: anti-Zionism, 'heritage American' framing, opposition to the 'woke right' label, and closer ties to America First.
