Daily Wire (Shapiro, Boreing, Walsh, Knowles, Klavan)

LAYER ORGANIZATION · FIRST SEEN 2026-11-04

Also listed as: MR: Daily Wire (Shapiro, Boreing, Walsh, Knowles, Klavan)

Active

The Daily Wire calls itself one of "America's fastest-growing media companies and counter-cultural outlets for news, opinion, and entertainment" and says it "does not claim to be without bias. We're opinionated, we're noisy, and we're having a good time."

Its About page (about[1]) says it was "Conceived by Ben Shapiro and Jeremy Boreing" as "something unique in the right-of-center media landscape — a truly for-profit business with an emphasis on distribution and marketing", launched in 2015 with Caleb Robinson; its DailyWire+ service carries The Daily Wire, Jordan Peterson, PragerU and Bentkey, and its podcast network makes "The Ben Shapiro Show," "The Matt Walsh Show," "The Michael Knowles Show" and "Morning Wire."

The company is owned by Bentkey Ventures, "majority owned by the original co-founders". Atlas notes: The Daily Wire company account and its founders and hosts, kept together as one single-outlet faction. Shapiro is also listed with the critics of the dissident / 'woke' right.
