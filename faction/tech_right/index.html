Tech Right

LAYER COALITION · ERA c. 2016–present · FIRST SEEN 2026-10-30

Active

Founders, investors and executives from big tech and venture capital who moved right, chiefly 2016-2024 (Thiel at the 2016 RNC; Musk, Andreessen, Horowitz, Sacks backing Trump in 2024; Chamath shifting to Republican donations from 2020-21) and took roles in the second Trump administration (Sacks as AI and crypto czar; Sriram Krishnan as senior AI policy advisor; Michael Kratsios at OSTP; Jacob Helberg at State; Ken Howery as ambassador; DOGE 2025-26).

Markers: deregulation and AI acceleration, anti-'woke AI', defence tech, crypto, high-skill immigration (which puts it at odds with H-1B restrictionists). "PayPal Mafia" is a common term, from a 2007 Fortune magazine feature, for a network of former PayPal employees and founders.

Several of them (Thiel, Musk, Sacks) are prominent in the Tech Right; others, including Reid Hoffman, are not [11][10]. Malcolm and Simone Collins have said in their own words that they previously worked on invite-only private networks (Dialog, a Schmidt Futures network they call Act 2, Future Forum, and others they will not name).

Those claims are recorded in extra.claimed_private_networks as claimed/unverified.
