Libertarians

LAYER COALITION · FIRST SEEN 2026-10-15

Active

The Libertarian Party, the Mises Institute and ancaps. The Mises Caucus has controlled the LP since 2022 and is contesting the 2026 LNC races.

Its paleo-libertarian wing (Dave Smith, Tom Woods) overlaps with the anti-war right. The wing split runs on two axes: (1) closeness to the DR and America First (paleo/pipeline and Mises Caucus) versus (2) classical and anti-Trump libertarians. Antiwar, party-organization and meme sub-wings sit between them.

The Libertarian Party has never been the home of all libertarians: long-running strategies include working through the Republican Party, agorism/counter-economics, and the Libertarian Party itself. Individuals move between strategies as circumstances change.
