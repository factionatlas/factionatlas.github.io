MAGA movement

LAYER COALITION · FIRST SEEN 2026-11-01

Also listed as: MR: MAGA movement

Active

The movement around Donald Trump and his slogan "Make America Great Again": loyalists, MAHA and election-integrity activists, and MAGA-dissident independents, carrying Tea Party activists and themes. Its defining statement is Trump's second inaugural address (2025-01-20, White House[2]), which opens "the golden age of America begins right now" and promises: "I will, very simply, put America first."

He calls his program "the complete restoration of America and the revolution of common sense", says "Our sovereignty will be reclaimed", and says he was "saved by God to make America great again."

Its named commitments include a "national emergency at our southern border", "drill, baby, drill", an order to "stop all government censorship and bring back free speech", a society "colorblind and merit-based", "only two genders: male and female", and a military that measures success by "the wars that we end — and perhaps most importantly, the wars we never get into."

The party platform carrying the slogan as its title, "Make America Great Again!"
