Israel: the Israeli government, its policy and US support

Stance on the Israeli government and its policy, and on US support for Israel (aid, arms, alliance). It cuts across the political tree: anti-aid voices sit in AF, CN, postliberal, libertarian, Tucker/Candace and even mainstream-right sub-factions, and pro-Israel voices sit in mainstream right, woke-right critics and Christian conservatives. Opposing the Israeli government never codes any of the other three.

These are tracked as four separate issues because they are different claims. Opposing the Israeli government or its policy is not the same as hostility to Judaism as a religion, which is not the same as claims about Jewish group influence or ethnic rivalry, which is not the same as calling for violence against Jews. A stance on one is never inferred from another, and every stance comes from the person's own words with a source link. See all four.
