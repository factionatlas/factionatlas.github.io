National identity: heritage vs creedal

Who counts as a member of the nation (US or UK). Heritage/ancestral: belonging rests on ancestry, ethnicity or birthright, and naturalized or second-generation citizens (often Indian Americans) are not fully American/British. Creedal/civic: belonging rests on shared principles, culture or faith, not ethnicity.
